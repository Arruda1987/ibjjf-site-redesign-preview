Academy Ranking Information

Athlete rankings are based on points that competitors receive from placing first, second, or third in IBJJF tournaments. Points from the current season and the previous two seasons are included. Points are only valid for the age division and belt rank where they are received.

Season weights and Grand Slam rules follow the official public ranking information pages on ibjjf.com.

Master divisions follow the same published point window, applied to the corresponding master age groups.

No-Gi rankings are calculated separately from Gi rankings, using results from official No-Gi championships.

Grand Slam ranking information is published on the official IBJJF ranking pages.

Kids ranking rules, where applicable, are described on the official ranking information page.
