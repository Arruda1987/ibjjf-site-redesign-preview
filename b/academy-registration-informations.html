Academy Registration Guide

How to register an academy

The Head Instructor must be a Black Belt registered with IBJJF. The academy registration fee is USD 70 and is valid for the calendar year. United States academies must complete a Background Check and SafeSport training as published on the official guide.

1

Start registration

Log in at the IBJJF membership system and select Register New Academy. Choose the country where the academy is located.

2

Association approval

If the academy is applying under an association, the association's representative must approve the request.

3

Assistant instructor confirmation

If assistant instructors are listed, the system will send them approval requests.

4

Payment hold

The Head Instructor must provide a credit card. A payment hold is placed (USD 70), but the fee is only charged after approval.

5

IBJJF review

IBJJF reviews the application. The academy name is analyzed according to the nomenclature rules.

6

Certificate issuance

Once approval is confirmed, payment is finalized and the printed academy certificate is mailed.

Academy name rules

Academy names must be distinguishable, follow the published formatting rules, and cannot use names that IBJJF has listed as rejected terms (for example generic martial-arts names used alone).
